For Students

From curious beginners to confident builders.

We work with school children across multiple levels — introducing them to technology the right way. Our programs are designed to meet students where they are, not where a textbook thinks they should be.

  • Primary Level (Class 3–4): Digital literacy, safe internet use, and creative computing with visual tools.
  • Lower Secondary (Class 5–7): How computers and the internet work, visual programming, and hands-on electronics.
  • Secondary (Class 8–10): Real programming, robotics, AI tools, and full-stack projects.
  • Higher Secondary (Class 11–12): Advanced software engineering, AI/ML fundamentals, and career-ready skills.

For Teachers

Empower the people who empower students.

Great technology education needs great teachers. We train school instructors in modern tools, classroom technology integration, and how to make technical subjects come alive for students of all ages.

  • Digital Pedagogy Workshops: How to teach programming, robotics, and AI effectively in a classroom.
  • Tool Familiarization: Hands-on sessions with the hardware and software your students will use.
  • Curriculum Co-design: Work with us to adapt our modules to your school's unique rhythm and needs.
  • Ongoing Mentorship: A direct line to our engineers when your teachers hit a technical roadblock.

Free Learning Resources

Good learning should not always need a price tag.

We maintain a growing collection of free, open resources for anyone who wants to learn — students, teachers, or self-learners. No catch, no sign-up walls, just good material.

  • Visual Explainer Series: Short, illustrated guides on how the internet, computers, and AI work.
  • Activity Templates: Downloadable worksheets and project ideas for classroom or self-study use.
  • Open Code Repositories: Sample projects, starter kits, and reference code for common concepts.
  • Community Sessions: Occasional free webinars and Q&A sessions on trending tech topics.
