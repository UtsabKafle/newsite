How Devices Connect to the Internet

A visual walkthrough of the path your data travels — from your device all the way to the global internet and back.

1 2 3 4
Device

Your Device

Any internet-connected device — laptop, phone, tablet, or desktop. It sends requests (like loading a webpage) as data packets through its network interface.

Router

Router

Your home or office router directs traffic between your local devices and the wider internet. It uses NAT (Network Address Translation) to manage multiple devices on a single connection.

ISP

ISP (Internet Service Provider)

Companies like Comcast, AT&T, or Verizon that own the physical infrastructure (fiber, coax, DSL) connecting your home network to the broader internet backbone.

Internet

The Internet

A global network of interconnected servers and data centers. Your request reaches its destination here — a website's server — which sends back the data you requested.
