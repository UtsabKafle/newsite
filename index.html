NEBians — Nepal's learning community

A live study network for Nepali students from Class 8 to Masters: searchable library of notes, past papers and SET solutions, an active discussion forum, NEB exam result checker, and Neby — an AI study buddy that answers questions inside discussions. Available on web and as a native Android app.

Roadmap

What we're building next

Neby, our study buddy inside NEBians, already answers questions and explains concepts to students every day. Next up: tutoring grounded in our own library, better Nepali-language support, and safe, curriculum-aligned answers.

Live today

Neby answers in the forum

Neby already participates in NEBians discussions — posting explanations, answering follow-ups, and keeping study threads moving for Classes 8–12 and beyond.

In progress

Curriculum-grounded tutoring

Step-by-step explanations aligned to the NEB syllabus, with retrieval over our own library so answers cite real notes and past papers instead of guessing.

Next

Nepali-first, safe by design

Mixed Nepali–English support for real classrooms, with teacher oversight, age-appropriate guardrails, and transparent AI labelling on every Neby post.
